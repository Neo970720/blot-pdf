1. What Blot is

Blot is a browser-based tool that removes content from PDF files by rasterising pages and painting redactions into them, then writes a new PDF. It runs on your device. We do not receive, store or process your documents.

4. Your responsibility for the redaction

This is the important part. A redaction is only as complete as the areas you marked. Blot's leak check reopens the file it produced, searches the text layer for the values it redacted and reports the result, but it cannot know what you forgot to mark, and it does not run OCR on scanned pages. Before you send a document to anyone, open the exported file yourself, search for the sensitive values, and check it in a reader other than the one that made it — the standard practice in legal and compliance work.
